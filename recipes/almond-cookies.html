Almond Cookies

Description

A recipe for delicious almond cookies.

Ingredients

  • 300g whole wheat flour
  • 200g room temperature butter
  • 50g almonds, broken
  • 50g sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon bicarbonate of soda

Steps

  1. Mix butter and sugar in a bowl with a wooden spoon until smooth.
  2. Sift flour and bicarbonate of soda into a separate bowl and mix together with salt.
  3. Transfer the flour mixture into the bowl containing the butter and sugar.
  4. Combine by rubbing together with fingers and knead until a dough forms.
  5. Add the almond pieces and knead until they are well dispersed in the dough.
  6. Roll the dough into a cylinder with a 4cm diameter.
  7. Place the dough on a tray and put in the fridge for 2 hours.
  8. Remove the dough cylinder from the fridge and cut into 1cm tall discs.
  9. Lay the discs onto an oven tray and place into a preheated oven at 160°C.
  10. Remove from the oven after 15 minutes and transfer to a cooling rack.
  11. Once cooled and hardened, serve with chai. Enjoy!
