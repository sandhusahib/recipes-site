Chai

Description

Chai is Indian spiced milk tea. This recipe makes 6 cups.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups filtered/mineral water
  • 3 cups unhomogenised organic whole milk
  • 3 tablespoons premium Assam tea leaves
  • 1 thumb fresh grated ginger
  • 1 teaspoon fennel seeds (saunf)
  • ½ teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 3 – 4 cloves (long)
  • 4 green cardamon pods (chhoti elaichi)
  • 3cm dried licorice root (muluthi)
  • 2cm cinnamon quil (dalchini)
  • 4 teaspoons organic raw sugar

Steps

  1. Bring the water to boil in a pan.
  2. Add the fennel and carom seeds to the pan.
  3. Pound the cloves, cardamon pods, licorice root and cinnamon using a pestle and mortar, then add these to the boiling water.
  4. After a minute add the ginger.
  5. After another minute add the tea leaves.
  6. After two more minutes add the milk.
  7. Bring to boil again.
  8. After two minutes of boiling, add the sugar.
  9. Boil for a further two minutes before serving – preferably with snacks (see here). Enjoy!
